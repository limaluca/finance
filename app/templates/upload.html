Import a screenshot

Take a screenshot of your credit card statement on your phone, AirDrop or copy it to this computer, then pick it here. Nothing is saved to the spending list until you review it on the next screen.

Screenshot

PNG, JPG, WEBP or HEIC. Large images take a couple of seconds.

The date matters. Bank notifications only say "Sun, 19:26", so the day the screenshot was taken is what turns that into a real date. Leave it as today if you just took the screenshot.

Tips for a clean read
  • Full-resolution screenshots work best — do not crop tightly or zoom out.
  • Only “Compra no crédito” notifications are imported. Promos and other apps in the same screenshot are ignored.
  • A notification whose title is hidden behind the card above it gets skipped, because there is no timestamp to read. Scroll so titles are visible.
  • Overlapping screenshots are fine — repeats are flagged as duplicates and left unchecked on the review screen.

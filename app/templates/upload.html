    PNG, JPG, WEBP or HEIC. Each image takes a second or two to read.

    What can be read
    • Purchase notifications — the lock-screen cards titled “Compra no crédito”. Promos and other apps are ignored. These carry a time of day.
    • The bank's statement list — the “Faturas” / “Despesas da fatura” screen. These carry the bank's own category and a real date, but no time.

    Overlapping screenshots are safe: repeats are detected and locked so the same purchase cannot be counted twice.

    Two things worth knowing
    • On the statement screen, make sure the day header (“Sexta, 11 de set”) is visible above the first purchase. Rows above the first header get a guessed date and have to be checked by hand.
    • The taken on date matters for notifications, which only say “Sun, 19:26”. Leave it as today if the screenshots are fresh.

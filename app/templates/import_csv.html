  • date is required, as YYYY-MM-DD.
  • merchant and amount are required. Everything else may be blank.
  • amount is positive for spending, negative for a refund. Both 34.55 and 34,55 are accepted.
  • time may be blank — statement rows have no time.
  • category is guessed if left blank.
  • Comma, semicolon and tab separated files all work. Column order does not matter, and Portuguese headers (data, valor, categoria) are understood too.

To get a file in this format, use Export CSV on the spending page.
